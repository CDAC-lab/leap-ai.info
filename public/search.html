What LEAP does

Four kinds of AI, one goal

Every capability is built in-house by the Centre for Data Analytics and Cognition, and tuned to the rhythms of a university campus — semesters, seasons and everything in between.

Predictive AI

Forecast

Anticipates energy demand and renewable generation for every building — from the next 15 minutes to the next 12 months.

Explainable AI

Explain

Delivers every alert with the evidence behind it, so facilities teams can see why — and act with confidence.

Generative AI

Converse

Lets any staff member ask questions in plain English: how is the campus tracking, what changed, what's possible.

Causal AI

Reason

Separates what changed from what caused it, so investments in equipment and upgrades are justified before they're made.

How it works

From sensor to action, every morning

LEAP runs continuously, refreshing every 15 minutes — so decisions are always based on what's happening now.

1

Sense

Meters, building systems, solar arrays, weather stations and satellites stream data from all five campuses.

2

Unify

200 data sources are brought together into one secure, governed data platform.

3

Analyse

Predictive, Explainable, Generative and Causal AI work together over the unified data.

4

Act

Clear, prioritised insights reach facilities, finance and academic teams — every weekday morning.
